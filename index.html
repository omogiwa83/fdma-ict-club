About Us

The FDMA ICT Club trains students in technology skills beyond the classroom. We focus on web development, graphic design, robotics, and digital literacy to help students achieve "Destiny Fulfilment" through technology.

What We Do

Coding & Web Design

Learn HTML, CSS, Python and build real websites and apps.

Robotics

Build and program robots using Arduino and sensors.

Graphics & Video

Learn Canva, Photoshop, and video editing for school projects.
